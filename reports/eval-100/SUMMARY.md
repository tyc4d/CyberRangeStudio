## Reading these results

These 100 reports are IC3/CISA CSA advisories. A spot check of the blocked cases shows most describe phishing, business email compromise, malware indicators (IOCs), YARA rules, or policy — not a specific vulnerable product and version. Such reports have no deployable vulnerable service by design, so the pipeline correctly resolves them to "no deployable target" instead of inventing a lab. The 2 deployable-lab cases and the 34 cases with online PoC candidates are the subset that names a concrete product or CVE (for example the two Iranian APT advisories).

So the low deployable rate reflects this corpus (fraud/IOC-heavy), not only the catalogue's reach. A CVE-focused corpus (for example CISA KEV or vendor vulnerability bulletins) would resolve a higher share. For the vuln-bearing subset the system builds a lab, and one such lab (Struts S2-045) was separately exploited live on the isolated PVE farm with a real OGNL payload.

One case (`csa-2022-220325-2`) was terminated by the run watchdog after its analysis call hung past 150 seconds; it is recorded as failed/incomplete, not as a result.

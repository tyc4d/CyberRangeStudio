# Security Incident Notice — Northfield Example Cooperative

Published: 14 March (year not stated)

Northfield Example Cooperative recently became aware of unusual activity affecting some of its IT systems. After detecting the activity, we took a number of systems offline as a precaution and engaged an external cybersecurity firm to assist with our investigation.

Based on the investigation to date, we believe an unauthorized party gained access to part of our network at some point in the past several weeks. The unauthorized party may have taken advantage of a weakness in an internet-facing system. While in our environment, the unauthorized party appears to have used legitimate administrative tools and may have accessed a number of internal servers.

We have reason to believe that some files were viewed or copied. We are still working to determine what information was involved and whose information it was. At this time we have no evidence that the information has been misused.

Some commentators on social media have suggested that this incident is connected to a well-known ransomware group. We are not able to confirm this. No systems were encrypted as far as we are aware.

We have reset passwords, applied available software updates, and put additional monitoring in place. Law enforcement has been notified. Our services are operating normally.

We take the security of our members' information very seriously and will provide updates as appropriate.
